What stays on your phone

Your music files, queue, equaliser settings, play counts, downloads, lyrics you save, and your listening stats are stored on your device. milo. does not upload your music files.

If you create an account

We store your email, username, display name, optional profile photo and cover, your liked songs, playlists and saved stations (so they sync between devices), your friends list, and chat messages you send. Chat messages are removed from the server after one day. Listen Together invites travel as chat messages. You choose what others can see in Profile → Sharing.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account removes your profile, synced library, friendships and messages and your login from our servers.

Permissions

Audio / storage: to find and play your music. Notifications: playback controls. Microphone: only when you record a voice note in chat. Bluetooth: to show and use your audio devices. Internet: radio, search, lyrics, sync and streaming.

Third-party services

Depending on what you use, milo. contacts: Supabase (account and sync), Google (sign-in, if you choose it), YouTube / YouTube Music (search and streaming), LRCLIB (lyrics), radio station servers, and optionally ListenBrainz or Last.fm (scrobbling, with credentials you enter; they stay on your device). Those services have their own policies.

What we do not do

No ads, no selling of data, no analytics profile of you.
